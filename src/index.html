Flight manual.

  1. Launch your flight. The countdown starts when you press Initiate Launch.
  2. Click or tap the flight area. Every input adds points and accelerates your ship. You can also press Space during a flight.
  3. Break through five stages. Reach 70, 180, 350 and 600 points before your ship’s timer runs out.
  4. Upgrade your ship. All flight points become credits after each run. Each ship has a different shape, speed gain and flight time. Cargo flies for 35 seconds at +1 speed per click; Stealth flies for 12 seconds at +5. Speed gain also determines points per click; raw click speed stays honest.

Velocity is an arcade simulation. Faster clicking adds thrust; engine momentum fades smoothly when you slow down.
